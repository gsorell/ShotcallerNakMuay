Or run it in order

When you want a sequence in your body, not a surprise.

Random is the default, not the only way. Switch the calls to sequential and the app reads a style straight through in order — which is how you take a long combination and drill it until you stop having to think about it.

Not beginner, intermediate, advanced

The styles are the real ones.

Muay Thai has always sorted fighters by how they win rather than by how long they've trained — the puncher, the kicker, the knee fighter, the technician. That's the framework this is built on, and every one of them carries its own list of combinations. Not one shared pool reshuffled under a different name.

Muay Mat

The puncher

Heavy hands, body work, and the low kick that comes off them.

Overhand, 4 to the Body, 3 to the Body

Muay Tae

The kicker

Long range, round kicks in series, teeps to hold the distance.

Right Low Kick, Right High Kick

Muay Khao

The knee fighter

Pressure, the clinch, and knees from every entry into it.

1 2, Clinch, Double Knees

Muay Femur

The technician

Read it, take it, make them pay — catches, sweeps and counters.

Catch Kick, 2 3, Right Knee, Sweep Right

It goes past the list. In Muay Tae the lead check comes up three times as often as a normal entry and the rear check twice, because a kicker's round is mostly checking — Muay Femur doubles both, the technician's defining habit. The frequencies are tuned style by style, so a round doesn't just use the right techniques, it feels like the fighter it's named after. Muay Sok is there for the elbows. Questions

The things people ask first.

What can I do for free, and what needs Pro?

Free: the round timer and every setting on it, three styles — Timer Only, Freestyle and Nak Muay Newb — the first three levels of Start Here, the jab and cross lessons, your workout log, and the completion card at the end of every session.

Pro: the other seventeen styles, the rest of the guided path, the full technique library, styles you build and edit yourself, the training options — southpaw, calisthenics and ordered callouts — and the charm ladder. It's bought in the app, through the App Store or Google Play.

Are the styles just names, or is the content actually different?

Different. Muay Khao is built around entries to the clinch and knees off them; Muay Tae runs kicks in series and teeps to hold range; Muay Mat is hands and the low kick that comes off them; Muay Femur is catches, sweeps and counters. The frequencies differ too — a Muay Tae round calls the lead check three times as often as a normal entry, because that's what a kicker's round is mostly made of.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. The native apps are fully offline. The web version caches itself on first visit, so once you've loaded it once, a dead gym basement is fine.

Will it talk over my music?

It plays alongside whatever you're listening to rather than stopping it. On Android, some device and headphone combinations handle the ducking better than others — if the balance is off, turn the call speed and volume up in settings.

Shadow boxing, the bag, or pad work?

All three. Shadow boxing suits faster calls and more movement; bagwork suits slower calls and longer combinations; on pads it calls for both of you, so the person holding isn't the one deciding what comes next either. There's a write-up on the difference.

I don't know any of these techniques yet. Is that a problem?

No — that's what the Start Here path is for. It begins at stance and the jab, introduces a couple of techniques at a time, and every one of them has a lesson with a reference figure attached. Start at level one and go.

I bought the app back when it was paid. What happens to me?

You keep everything. Open the app and it restores your access on its own; if it doesn't, there's a manual claim on the plan screen.
